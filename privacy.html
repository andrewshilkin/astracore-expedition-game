Privacy Policy

Game: Expedition · Developer: Iron Parrot Games · Last updated: 14 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the game Expedition ("the game", "we") processes when you play it, and why. We do not ask for, or knowingly collect, your name, email address, or any account credentials. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Data we process

  • Gameplay & usage analytics. We use the third-party ByteBrew analytics SDK to understand how the game is played (for example: sessions started, levels completed, and which menus/screens are opened) and to diagnose crashes and errors. This is collected together with a randomly generated, non-identifying device/session identifier and basic technical information (such as device type and browser). It is not linked to your real-world identity.
  • Platform data (CrazyGames). When the game is played on CrazyGames, the CrazyGames SDK and platform process data as described in the CrazyGames privacy policy (for example, to serve advertising and measure engagement).
  • Advertising. The game shows interstitial and rewarded video ads through the CrazyGames platform. Advertising partners may process data in accordance with their own privacy policies.
  • Local device storage. Your game progress (save data) and settings (such as volume) are stored locally on your own device via browser storage. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

2. Why we process it

To operate and improve the game, understand aggregate player behavior, fix bugs and crashes, and — on supported platforms — to display advertising that keeps the game free to play.

3. Third parties

  • ByteBrew (analytics) — ByteBrew privacy policy
  • CrazyGames (platform & ads) — CrazyGames privacy policy

4. Children

The game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information that identifies a child. The analytics described above use only non-identifying, aggregate data.

5. Your choices

You can clear the locally stored save/settings at any time by clearing your browser's site data. If you play on a platform such as CrazyGames, that platform may also offer audio, privacy, or consent controls in its own interface.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at contact.ironparrot.games@gmail.com.
